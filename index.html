The Philosophy • Bandra West

Where Ancient Indian Terroir Meets Modern Avant-Garde

Conceived by Master Chef Julian Laurent and culinary visionary Chef Aarav Sen, AURA reimagines the subcontinent’s rich culinary traditions through modern gastronomy, heirloom wood-smoke techniques, and world-class sommelier pairings.

Each morning begins with cold-pressed oils from Malabar, saffron strands flown from Kashmir, truffles from Umbria, and sea-catch fresh off the Arabian coastline.

The Culinary Manifesto

"Gastronomy is storytelling through scent, fire, memory, and geometry."

At AURA Bandra, we dismantle the barriers between traditional royal Mughlai kitchens, coastal Malabar secrets, and cutting-edge French gastronomy. Our curries slow-simmer for 36 hours over aged tamarind wood coals, while our breads are baked to order with imported Italian winter black truffles.

We work directly with certified organic farmer cooperatives across Maharashtra, Kerala, and Kashmir — ensuring complete purity, zero artificial additives, and maximum depth of flavor in every bite.
